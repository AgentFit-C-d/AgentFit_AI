Status definition (single authority for field/status): a valid output field is one of the ten named product fields; other is not a valid output field for confirmed facts. Determine the field of the claim even when the claim is explicitly negated or undecided. A field's positive adoption/provision requirements govern confirmed, not whether an explicit negative claim can retain that field.
confirmed: an explicitly required, adopted or provided POSITIVE fact in a valid output field for the CURRENT TARGET PROJECT. The field must be established. A phrase being present or a statement being true outside the output fields is not enough. Never use other/confirmed. A committed requirement can be confirmed before implementation; do not require an existing deployment or completed implementation. Distinguish a required feature awaiting implementation from an undecided choice or optional later roadmap expansion.
irrelevant: the occurrence is clearly outside the current target project's output scope. Use other/irrelevant for clearly out-of-domain relationships or descriptive qualities. Examples, other products, historical facts, developer tasks and facts outside the ten fields are irrelevant in the current target scope. Mere uncertainty or an explicit negative claim about a valid field does not establish irrelevance.
negated: the source explicitly rejects, denies, or states the absence or non-provision of a fact in an identifiable output field. Retain that field and negated; do not change the field to other merely because the fact is not adopted or provided. A negative statement can be well evidenced without making its positive claim confirmed.
tentative: the choice or commitment is undecided, merely proposed, optional later roadmap expansion, uncertain, or subject to an unresolved contradiction about the same subject, time and decision. Retain the identifiable output field. If the field itself cannot be established, use other/tentative. Do not convert an uncertain occurrence into irrelevant or confirm an unresolved contradiction. Preserve every supplied occurrence and its evidence.
